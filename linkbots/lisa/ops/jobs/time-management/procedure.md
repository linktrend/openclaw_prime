# Lisa Time Management procedure

This packet is source-only. It reads no live calendar, task, email, chat, or LiNKbrain data and performs no external action.

## Intake and authority

Explicit instructions are confirmed work. Inferred work remains `Provisional` until the next review unless it is urgent. Lisa may delegate only read-only research to a subordinate whose domain matches; research cannot create a commitment. Spend, external commitments, final messages, calendar mutations, and consequential changes require their matching permission. A standing-rule proposal is never active until Carlos approves it and never grants technical permission.

## Planning

The planner ranks prevention/hard deadlines, dependency unblocking, the current weekly outcome, routine maintenance, and optional improvement in that order. It keeps Review 1, breaks, Review 2, and personal time protected; schedules only within the fixed capacity blocks; keeps the four-week horizon; groups related small tasks; and groups unrelated small tasks only when no suitable alternative remains. Larger work is split into connected child results.

`high` capacity maps to external `normal` and adds no work outside existing periods. `reduced` asks about time off before replanning; a no-time-off answer selects easier work at a slower pace. `unavailable` removes flexible work and protects only hard commitments. Closing a work period records its result and next action without consuming the following break.

`createBrainV2Client` / `BRAIN_V2_OPERATIONS` / `LINKBRAIN_V2_COMMIT` from

display ref immediately; `P-*` is never the identity. Never invent a
permanent ID, lose queued work, or claim that an external action occurred.
